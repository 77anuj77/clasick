FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before you drop in a resume.

Is my resume uploaded anywhere?

No. Parsing runs in your browser — your file is read locally and never leaves your device. Nothing is stored on our servers.

What is an ATS score?

An applicant tracking system ranks resumes against a role before a recruiter sees them. Your score reflects how well your resume matches the job description you paste — keywords, sections, formatting, and impact.

Can I edit the resume after the score?

Yes. Clasick pairs the analysis with an editor, AI rewrite suggestions, and a prioritized action plan, so you can fix what matters and re-score in the same session.

Which formats can I export?

PDF and DOCX. Edits round-trip to real Word files and PDFs with the layout you see on the A4 canvas.

Do you need an account?

No account is needed to try the live demo. Parsing is free and local; nothing is stored.

Is Clasick open source?

Yes — the full project is on GitHub, and you can run the complete analysis workflow locally on your own machine.
